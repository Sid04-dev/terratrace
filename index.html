      About
      
            TerraTrace
            
      
            The problem: NASA's Earth data is free, but most students can't use it: it needs coding, GIS software and statistics. So assignments end up citing blogs instead of evidence.
            
      
            Our answer: click a place → get a trend that has been statistically tested (Mann–Kendall + Sen's slope, the methods climate scientists use), explained in plain language, ready to cite.
